How this works. Words arrive in frequency order because coverage of real text is wildly front-loaded: the first hundred lemmas carry more running text than the next nine hundred put together. Each word is drilled by retrieval, never by re-reading, and the schedule expands only as long as you keep getting it right. Recognition unlocks production, production unlocks sentences and audio — because being able to read a word and being able to say it are different skills, trained in that order.

Coverage percentages are estimates interpolated from published lemma-frequency data for large Spanish corpora. Everyday speech runs higher than these figures, technical prose lower. Progress is stored in this browser only.
